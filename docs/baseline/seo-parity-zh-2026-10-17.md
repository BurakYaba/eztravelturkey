# SEO parity with the old Chinese site (2026-10-17)

P5.9. The new site against the old Chinese site as archived in October 2026 (`data/inventory/seo-zh.csv`). Titles and descriptions are compared with the text the import started from, so a difference here is a change made on purpose or a bug. Pages are joined by their address without the domain. For Chinese and Japanese the word count is a character count (there are no spaces between words). Generated by `scripts/qa/parity.mjs zh` from a crawl of the site (`scripts/qa/crawl.mjs`).

## Coverage

- Old Chinese pages answering 200 (without /cn/): 232. On the new site at the same address: **71**. Not on the new site: 161. New addresses that the old site did not have: 6.
- Not on the new site at the same address, by kind: page 5, static 1, home 1, tab of a tour 154. Every one of them has a fate in `data/mapping/redirects-zh.csv`: the tabs of a tour (/inclusion-rate/, /review/ ... were empty pages) and the pages whose address changed get a 301 to the page that took their place (P7.5).
- Fate of those 161: 301-page 158, 301-parent 1, same 2.
- New addresses: /zh/, /zh/multi-country-tours/, /zh/plan-my-trip/, /zh/private-tours/, /zh/small-group-tours/, /zh/tours/.

## What changed on purpose

- A title that was only the site name ("Eztravel Turkey" on seven Chinese pages) or that belonged to another page (the Japanese privacy page carried the FAQ title) became the page heading followed by the site name. The hub pages, which were the home page under another address, take the menu word as heading. Every other title, description and heading is the old one.
- Chinese and Japanese descriptions are kept whatever their length (the 120 to 160 characters of Ek H.3 are for Latin letters). A few tours have a description under 20 characters; it is the old text and a task for a native editor.
- Israel: nothing that names the country is carried over (`以色列`, `イスラエル`). The Chinese FAQ had none; the Japanese FAQ lost one question. Six Chinese Jordan tours mention the 6th-century Madaba map of Jerusalem, which is Jordan content and stays.
- The tab addresses of every tour (`/inclusion-rate/`, `/review/`, `/reviews/`, `/tour-itinerary/`, `/itinerary/`) answered 200 with an empty page and a meaningless title; they redirect to the tour (P7.5).
- The Japanese folder `/jp/目的地/ギリシャツアー /` had a blank at the end of its name; the new address has none and the old one redirects.
- Text pages and the home page are the old text inside the new design. The fixed words of the new design (menus, buttons, forms, section headings) are drafts that a native speaker still has to review (P5.7).
- Tours show their price tables and notes as the old tab did. The euro starting price of a Japanese tour is shown where the old page printed one; some Chinese price tables are for 2023 and 2024 (the old text): the client has to update them.
