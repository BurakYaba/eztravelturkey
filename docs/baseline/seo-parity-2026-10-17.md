The old tour pages carried `Product`, `AggregateRating`, `Offer`, `MerchantReturnPolicy` and `OfferShippingDetails` next to `TouristTrip` (Ek I). The new ones carry `TouristTrip` with an `Offer` per currency, `WebPage`, `BreadcrumbList` and, where the tour has questions, `FAQPage`. The self-made rating and the shipping and return policy of a tour are left out on purpose (D16): a company rating written by the company itself is not allowed in Google rich results, and a tour has no shipping. This is the one place where the new pages carry **less** structured data than the old ones.

## What changed on purpose

- Home page title (see above). Everything else keeps the old title, H1 and canonical.
- Two descriptions differ: the About page (the old one was 195 characters, over the 160 limit of Ek H.3, so a 141-character one written in code is used) and the destinations hub (the old text listed Israel; it now names the five countries).
- About, FAQ, Contact, Destinations hub and the home FAQ show the text of the old pages (from the CMS) inside the new design; the first draft had hand-written text there and 3 of these pages had different titles and about a third fewer words. Sections that mention Israel are left out. This is why the destinations hub has 927 words against 1350 (about 30% of the old text was Israel, and the card paragraphs of the old hub are now the country and combination tiles).
- Old pages not on the new site apart from blog posts: `/en/search/` (noindex, not in the sitemap) and `/en/videos/` (301 to the blog, D18).
- Three H1 elements per page became one.
- Pages that were thin or broken on the old site (two tours with a 13 and 32 character description) keep the old text; fixing them is a content decision (S2.2).
- Tours show a price in euro and dollar when both are entered; the old pages showed euro only.
- Country pages with category pages no longer list every tour (D42); Egypt, Jordan and Morocco got category pages (7 new addresses).
