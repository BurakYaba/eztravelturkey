**The short answer:** allow at least three nights. Spend one day in the Old City, one on the Bosphorus and the bazaars, and keep a flexible day for what you enjoy most.

## The Old City (Sultanahmet)

This is where most of the great monuments are within walking distance: Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Hippodrome, Topkapi Palace and the Basilica Cistern. Start early to avoid queues. Visiting arrangements at some sites change, so we confirm current rules before your tour.

## The bazaars

The Grand Bazaar is a covered maze of shops. The Spice Bazaar is smaller and more fragrant. Both are fun to explore with a guide who can point out good places to buy and eat.

## The Bosphorus

A cruise between Europe and Asia shows the city's waterfront palaces, wooden villas and fortresses. It is relaxing after a morning of sightseeing.

## Where to stay

Sultanahmet puts you next to the major sites. Other neighborhoods offer a more local feel. We choose hotels based on your priorities.

## Getting around

Trams, ferries and metro lines are efficient, and private transfers are simple to arrange. Our tours include airport and hotel transfers.

## Practical tips

- Dress modestly for mosques and carry a scarf if needed.
- Carry some cash for small purchases.
- Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons.

Istanbul is the starting point of most Turkey tours, followed by Cappadocia and the Aegean coast.
