**The short answer:** seven days covers Jordan well. Spend one full day in Petra, an evening and morning in Wadi Rum, and use the rest for Amman, Jerash and the King's Highway.

## A suggested route

1. **Amman.** Arrive and settle in.
2. **The north.** Visit Umm Qais, Jerash with its Roman streets and temples, and Ajloun Castle.
3. **Desert Castles.** Qusayr Amra has early Islamic frescoes, and there are fortresses at Kharana and Azraq.
4. **King's Highway.** Mount Nebo, the mosaic map at Madaba and Kerak Castle on the way south.
5. **Petra.** Walk the Siq to the Treasury, then go further to tombs, temples and viewpoints.
6. **Wadi Rum.** A jeep safari through canyons and dunes.

## Petra tips

- Allow a full day. The Treasury is the start of the site.
- Wear sturdy shoes and bring water.
- Start early to enjoy the Siq before the crowds.

## The Dead Sea

You can float in the water at the lowest point on land. It is a relaxed addition at the beginning or end of a trip.

## When to go

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable. Summer is hot in the south and winter can be cold at night, particularly in the desert.
