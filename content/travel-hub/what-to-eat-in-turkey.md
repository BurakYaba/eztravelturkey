**The short answer:** start the day with a Turkish breakfast, share meze at lunch, and try the regional dish of wherever you are.

## Breakfast

A Turkish breakfast is a spread of cheeses, olives, tomatoes, cucumbers, eggs, jams and fresh bread, served with plenty of tea. It is often the best meal of the day.

## Meze and mains

Meze are small plates, hot and cold, shared at the table. Kebabs come in many styles, and pide, a boat-shaped bread with toppings, is a good lunch.

## Regional specialties

- **Cappadocia:** testi kebab, a stew cooked in a sealed clay pot and broken open at the table.
- **The Aegean coast:** seafood and dishes with olive oil and fresh herbs.
- **Istanbul:** street food such as simit, a sesame bread ring, and fresh fish by the water.

## Sweets and drinks

Try baklava, Turkish delight and kunefe. Turkish tea is served in small tulip-shaped glasses all day, and Turkish coffee is strong and served with the grounds.
