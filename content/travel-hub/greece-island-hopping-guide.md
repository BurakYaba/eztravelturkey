**The short answer:** for a first trip, combine Athens with Mykonos and Santorini, give each island at least two nights, and travel by high-speed ferry.

## Choosing islands

- **Mykonos** is known for its windmills, Mykonos Town and lively beaches. Delos, an ancient sacred island, is a short boat ride away.
- **Santorini** is known for its caldera views, whitewashed villages and sunsets. The walk from Fira to Oia is a classic.
- **Naxos and Paros** are quieter, with long beaches and village life, and make a good addition on a longer trip.

## Ferries

Most Cyclades routes leave from Piraeus, Athens. High-speed boats are faster, while conventional ferries are slower and cheaper. Journey times vary widely by vessel and route. Our island packages include the speed ferry tickets and the transfers to and from each port.

## Weather and timing

The main season runs from April to mid October. The warmest sea is in July to September. In summer, strong winds can disrupt sailings, so avoid tight connections on the day you fly home.

## How long to stay

Eight days comfortably covers Athens, Mykonos and Santorini. Add three days or more to include a second pair of islands.

## Tips

- Spend one guided half day in Athens for the Acropolis, then switch to free time.
- Book sunset-view hotels early.
- Pack light, as island transfers involve steps and cobbled lanes.
