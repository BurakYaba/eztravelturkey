**The short answer:** plan two to three nights in Cappadocia. That is enough for the main valleys, an underground city and a balloon flight if the weather allows.

## Getting there

The easiest way is a domestic flight from Istanbul to Kayseri or Nevsehir, followed by a transfer of about an hour. Flying saves a long overland day, which is why our Turkey tours use it.

## What to see

- **Goreme and the valleys.** Walk or drive through Rose Valley, Love Valley and Pasabag, where the rock formations known as fairy chimneys stand in groups.
- **Uchisar Castle.** A rock citadel with wide views over the region.
- **Zelve Open Air Museum.** An abandoned cave settlement you can explore on foot.
- **Underground cities.** Kaymakli is one of the best known, with narrow passages and rooms carved over several levels.

## Hot air balloons

Flights take off at dawn and last about an hour. They depend on wind and weather, so cancellations happen. Book early in peak season and keep a spare morning if you can. A balloon ride is an optional extra on our tours.

## Where to stay

Many travelers choose a cave hotel for the experience. We select hotels by location, comfort and cleanliness, and can arrange a cave-style stay.

## Practical tips

- Wear comfortable shoes with grip, as paths are uneven.
- Dress in layers. Mornings are cool, even in summer.
- Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons.
