**The short answer:** April to June and September to October are the best months for a sightseeing tour of Turkey. Days are warm rather than hot, the light is good for photography, and ancient sites are comfortable to walk.

## Spring: April to June

Spring is green and mild. Istanbul is at its best, and the Aegean coast around Ephesus and Pamukkale is pleasant for walking. In April the city is known for its tulips. Evenings in Cappadocia can still be cool, so bring a light jacket.

## Summer: July and August

Summer is hot, especially inland and along the Aegean. It is also the busiest period. If you travel in these months, plan sightseeing for the early morning and late afternoon, and build in time for the coast or a hotel pool in the middle of the day.

## Autumn: September to October

Autumn is the second sweet spot. The heat has eased, the sea is still warm, and crowds thin out. Cappadocia looks golden, and conditions for ballooning are often good.

## Winter: November to March

Winter is quiet. Istanbul is cool and often grey, but museums are uncrowded. Cappadocia can be snowy, which many travelers find magical, though some activities run less often. Pamukkale and the coast are mild.

## Choosing by interest

- **Istanbul and culture:** spring or autumn.
- **Cappadocia ballooning:** spring and autumn, with a spare morning in case of wind.
- **Ephesus and Pamukkale:** spring and autumn for comfortable walking.
- **Fewer crowds and lower prices:** the edges of the season, or winter.
