**The short answer:** go between October and April, allow about a week for Cairo, a Nile cruise and Luxor, and arrange visas before you travel.

## Entry and visas

Entry rules depend on your nationality and can change. Visa fees are not included in our tours, so please check the current requirements for your passport before booking. Your passport should be valid for at least 6 months.

## When to go

October to April is cooler and more comfortable for sightseeing. Summer is very hot, especially in Luxor and Aswan. Tours run daily all year.

## How the trip works

A classic first visit combines Cairo with the Nile. You see the pyramids of Giza, the Great Sphinx, Memphis and Saqqara, then fly to Aswan for a three-night cruise to Luxor. Along the way you visit Philae, Kom Ombo and Edfu, and in Luxor the Valley of the Kings, Karnak and Luxor Temple. The Grand Egyptian Museum rounds out the trip in Cairo.

## Abu Simbel

The temples of Ramses II are a long early-morning excursion from Aswan, offered as an optional extra.

## What to pack

- Light, breathable clothing that covers shoulders and knees at religious sites
- A hat, sunglasses and sunscreen
- Comfortable walking shoes
- A refillable water bottle

## Tipping and money

Tipping is customary for guides, drivers and staff, and is not included in tour prices. Carry small notes.

## Guides

Our guides are Egyptologists who speak English or Spanish. They bring the temples and tombs to life far better than a guidebook can.
