**The short answer:** Morocco is best planned around three or four bases. Choose from Marrakech, Fes, Chefchaouen and the Sahara, and allow time to travel between them.

## The main stops

- **Marrakech.** The medina, the souks and the Koutoubia Mosque.
- **Fes.** One of the oldest medinas in the Arab world, with workshops and tanneries.
- **Chefchaouen.** A mountain town with blue-washed lanes.
- **The Sahara.** Dunes and camel rides near Merzouga.

## Travel times

Distances in Morocco are larger than they look on a map, and mountain roads are slow. Build rest days into the plan, or use private transfers with a driver so the journey is part of the experience.

## When to go

Spring and autumn suit most routes. Summer is very hot in the interior and the desert. If you travel during Ramadan, opening hours change, so ask us how this would affect your plans.

## Etiquette

- Dress modestly, especially away from the main tourist areas.
- Bargaining is normal in the souks. Be friendly and unhurried.
- Ask before photographing people.
